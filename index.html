01 / The idea

A studio by the sea.
Five times, the doors open.

This summer, ANTENA moves its creative studio to José Ignacio for one month dedicated to music production, experimentation and artistic exchange. Five times during the residency, we open the doors and invite people into this universe through intimate musical gatherings, live performances, listening sessions and encounters around music, gastronomy and design.

Not just another summer party.
A space to create, connect
and share the process.
